03 / Good questions
Is Common Ground a real business?

No. This is an original, self-initiated demonstration created by Radu Studio. It explores how a local service business can present its offer clearly and distinctively. No services, client projects, or outcomes are claimed.

Can Radu make a website for my business?

Yes. Radu Studio designs and develops websites for businesses in Finland and internationally. Tell the studio about your business and what you need; you will receive a clear next step.

Where did the garden illustration come from?

The illustration was created specifically for this concept using vector shapes. The palette, identity, copy, and page composition are original design explorations by Radu Studio.
