# Radu Studio identity

**Online Architecture** is the organizing thought: a distinctive place for a business online, designed and built with intent. The modular R represents structure that can take different forms. Warmth comes from the typography and mineral palette, rather than literal architectural imagery.

## Reusable files

- `assets/brand/radu-wordmark.svg`: outlined wordmark; no font dependency.
- `assets/brand/radu-mark.svg`: compact one-color R symbol.
- `assets/brand/radu-sculpture.svg`: six-part vermilion display mark.
- `assets/brand/radu-modules.svg`: flat component study for supporting compositions.
- `assets/favicon.svg`: small-format site icon.
- `assets/social.jpg`: 1200×630 social image.
- `docs/brand-sheet.html`: self-contained reference sheet using local assets.

The symbol, sculpture and module drawing are original graphics made for this project. No registered-trademark status is claimed.

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Mineral paper | `#EEEAE1` | Main canvas, reverse text |
| Ink | `#25251F` | Main text, buttons, footer |
| Vermilion | `#DF4929` | Signature sculpture, large contact area |
| Signal red | `#B83B22` | Small signal marks, focus and validation |
| Studio green | `#30382F` | Studio section |
| Sage | `#BAC1A5` | Supporting modules on studio green |
| Rule | `#C8C3B8` | Quiet dividers |

Use `#10100C` for small text on vermilion. Do not use vermilion as small text on paper. Prefer one dominant field of color, not multicolor panels. Existing concept identities belong to the fictional concepts and are not Radu brand colors.

## Typography

Instrument Sans (400–700) is the clear structural voice. Instrument Serif Italic adds warmth and tension at selected moments. Monospace annotations are sparing navigational details, not body copy. Fonts are self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License; licenses accompany the assets.

Use generous headline scale with deliberate line breaks. Avoid repeating a sans/italic pairing in every section. For proposals, use Instrument Sans body text at a comfortable reading size, with the serif reserved for brief opening statements.

## Composition and motion

Build on a strong baseline, deliberate asymmetry and generous spaces. The logo should have clear space of at least half its cap height. Never stretch it. The compact mark is suitable at 24px or larger; use the full wordmark when it has room to breathe.

Motion follows an assembly grammar: enter, align, separate, recombine. The sculpture has three authored states: Structure, Expression, Components. Main content does not move in response to its control. Entrance motion lasts 800ms; small hover feedback lasts 200–300ms. All essential content is readable without JavaScript, and reduced-motion preference suppresses motion.

## Voice

Plain service nouns. Short, useful sentences. Quiet personality. State deliverables and decisions; avoid guaranteed growth or invented scale. Say founder-led when describing the relationship. Use `team@radu.studio`.

Always label a fictional project or uncommissioned study in its card, detail page and working demo. Do not reuse concept businesses as client logos.
