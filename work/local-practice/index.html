The design question

How could a small landscape practice make its services understandable, feel worth considering, and help a homeowner take the first step?

The decisions

A direct service proposition meets a strong visual identity. Short service descriptions explain the proposed offer, with an enquiry link back to Radu and a stacked layout on small screens.

  • Plain-language service sections answer what the practice does before asking a homeowner to make contact.
  • A custom geometric garden illustration gives the service a recognisable identity without relying on invented project photographs.
  • A high-contrast link back to Radu and a stacked mobile layout keep the demonstration easy to explore.
Built for this study

A bespoke garden illustration, a responsive single-page service website, clear service sections and an enquiry link to Radu. This is a visual service-business demonstration; there are no real customers or project results.

A NOTE ON THIS WORK

This is a self-initiated design and development concept for a fictional landscape practice. It is a demonstration of approach, not a paid client project. No client relationship or commercial results are implied.
