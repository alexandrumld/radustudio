The design question

How could a fictional northern retreat convey a sense of place through landscape, typography and a readable mobile layout?

The decisions

An editorial rhythm, generous typography and a quiet landscape create a sense of place. Short, structured sections introduce the idea of a stay, then lead visitors to a clearly labeled Radu project enquiry.

  • Landscape before amenities: the opening gives a visitor a reason to imagine being there.
  • A restrained serif and short editorial chapters create a slower reading pace without hiding content behind motion.
  • The enquiry link stays in the navigation; on a phone, the headline and landscape are recomposed for a narrow screen.
Built for this study

Editorial art direction, a responsive single-page concept, locally served typography and images, and an enquiry link to Radu. This is a visual hospitality demonstration; there is no booking engine or real property.

A NOTE ON THIS WORK

This is a self-initiated design and development concept for a fictional retreat. It was created to explore a hospitality website, not commissioned by a client. No bookings, conversion results or client outcomes are claimed.
