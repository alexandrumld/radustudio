A few practical things

Before we begin.

What does a project cost?＋

It depends on the size of the website, the content and any integrations. After an initial conversation, we give you a clear proposal with a fixed fee. Share a rough budget if you have one; it helps us recommend a sensible scope.

How long will it take?＋

We agree a schedule once the scope and content are clear. The number of pages, feedback rounds and any specialist functionality all affect the timeline. Let us know early if you have a launch date in mind.

Can you improve an existing website?＋

Yes. We can review what is already there and recommend focused improvements or a rebuild. We will explain which approach makes sense before you commit.

Do I need to have everything ready?＋

No. A short description of your business and what you want to change is enough to begin. We will identify the content, images and decisions needed as part of the scope.
