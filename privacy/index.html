Radu Studio / Privacy

A little less data.
A little more clarity.

This website uses no advertising trackers, analytics scripts or cookies set by Radu.

When you enquire

The enquiry form runs in your browser. It prepares a message for your email app; it does not send your details to a website server or store them in your browser. You can also write directly to team@radu.studio.

If you send an email, Radu uses your name, email address and project details to respond and discuss a possible project. This is to take steps at your request before a potential contract. Email is handled by the email providers involved.

What to include

Please share only what is needed to discuss the project. Do not include sensitive personal information, passwords or confidential customer data.

Hosting and technical information

The hosting provider may process standard technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to serve and protect the website. Fonts and images used on the Radu pages are served locally; the page does not request them from a third-party font or image service.

Your choices

You can ask about, correct or request deletion of information you have sent to Radu by emailing team@radu.studio. Enquiry correspondence is kept only as long as needed for the discussion and any resulting work or applicable obligations.
